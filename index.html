The open-source stack

No single free tool replaces Capital IQ. A small stack covers most of what analysts use day to day.

ToolWhat it gives youLicenceWatch out for
OpenBB PlatformOpen-source research platform. One Python interface over many data providers, plus a terminal and workspace.AGPL-3.0Quality depends on the underlying provider; premium providers need their own keys.
SEC EDGARThe primary source: filings, XBRL financial facts, insider and ownership forms for US-listed companies.Public dataRaw and unstandardised. US filers only. Fair-access rate limits apply.

What you give up

Be honest about the gap before you switch.

Standardised, restated, point-in-time financialsYesDIY

Global coverage and ownershipYesUS-strong

Rule of thumb: open data is good enough for learning, screening, public-company valuation and first drafts. Pay for a terminal when you need clean estimates, private-market data or an audit trail your clients will accept.

Discounted cash flow

Value today = present value of future free cash flow plus terminal value.

Sensitivity: value per share

WACC (rows) against terminal growth (columns). Most of the value sits in the terminal assumption, which is why this table matters.

Cost of capital: CAPM and WACC

Cost of equity = risk-free + beta × equity risk premium. WACC weights it against after-tax debt.

DuPont analysis

ROE = net margin × asset turnover × financial leverage. Shows whether returns come from profitability, efficiency or debt.
